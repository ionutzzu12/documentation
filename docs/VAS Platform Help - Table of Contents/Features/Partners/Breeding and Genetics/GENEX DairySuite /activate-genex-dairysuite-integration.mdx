<ExpandableHeading>
  ### Required Permissions

  Only users who are assigned a Role with Integrations Management permissions can connect partner integrations. See [Edit Dairy User Permissions](../../Platform/Features/Config/UserMgmtRoles/EditUserPermissions.htm) and [Manage Dairy Roles](../../Platform/Features/Config/UserMgmtRoles/ManageRoles.htm) for more information.
</ExpandableHeading>

Follow these steps to configure and integrate \{\{UDS-general.GENEX-Pulse}} with \{\{General.CompanyName-short}} and provide \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.c-onsultants}} view-only access to your \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.h-erd}} data in \{\{masterVAS-Products.Pulse-only}}.

<ExpandableHeading>
  ## Step 1: Add Animal Parentage Data

  For the most accurate and comprehensive \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.h-erd}} analysis, be sure to add parentage details for the animals in your \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.h-erd}}. \{\{General.CompanyName-short}} recommends adding the data for all animals for the following standard \{\{masterVAS-Products.Pulse-only}}/\{\{DC-UI.DC-ProductName}} items: 

  - \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.S-ire-}} (\{\{SID}})
  - \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.D-am-}} (\{\{DID}})
  - Maternal \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.g-rands-ire}} (MGS)
  - Maternal great-\{\{masterVAS-Terminology.g-rands-ire}} (MGGS)

  This data is used to assess the \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.h-erd}}'s genomic profile and is the basis of calculations made for specific animal traits and potential improvements.

  The breed average is also incorporated into the assessment calculations, but having specific parentage data for the animals in your \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.h-erd}} greatly increases the accuracy of the analysis process:

  See [View Animal Details on CowCards](../../Platform/Features/CowCards/WorkingWithCowCards.htm) to learn more about viewing and adding animal item data.
</ExpandableHeading>

<ExpandableHeading>
  ## Step 2: Connect Integration in PULSE

  Follow these steps to establish the connection: 

  1. In \{\{masterVAS-Products.Pulse-only}}, navigate to **Partners&#x20;**>**&#xA0;Integrations Gallery&#x20;**>**&#xA0;**\{\{Partners.PartnerName}}.
  2. \{\{Commands.Click}}**See Details** or **See Requirements** on the \{\{Partners.PartnerName}}integration card.
  3. Follow the Connection Instructions on the right side of the page to establish the connection:1) Click the **Connection Status** toggle and verify the status switches to **Active**.:inlineImage[]{src="https://archbee-image-uploads-qa.s3.amazonaws.com/QjalyvC_Eyr95bGir3p2_/_BMD2SCmSlyfYkEEdQjjE_integration-connected.png" alt="Connection Status: Active" caption size="22"}
     2\) \{\{Commands.Click}}**Save**.
     3\) If necessary, \{\{Commands.click}}**Edit Integration** to make any adjustments or edits and save your changes.

  <hint type="warning">
    **IMPORTANT:&#x20;**&#x49;f your integration does not automatically connect, contact your \{\{Partners.PartnerName}} representative to connect the integration from their system.
  </hint>
</ExpandableHeading>

<ExpandableHeading>
  ## Step 3: Work with Your Genetics Consultant to Build a Genetic Plan

  Once the data connection is active, your genetic \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.c-onsultant}} will contact you to begin reviewing your \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.h-erd}}'s genomic data and analyzing options to improve specific genomic traits of your \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.h-erd}}. You will work together to customize and implement the plan, which includes \{\{masterVAS-Terminology.s-ire}} selection and animal mating recommendations based on your genetic goals.
</ExpandableHeading>
